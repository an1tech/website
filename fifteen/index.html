The classic fifteen puzzle

Four boards, from Easy 3×3 to Expert 6×6. Every shuffled board can be solved. Games are saved automatically, and your statistics stay on your device.

Need help? Email support@an1.tech. Please include your app version from Settings → Version.

Questions

Where is my progress stored?

On your device only. Your current game, statistics and settings are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I unlock bigger boards?

Easy (3×3) and Classic (4×4) are open from the start. Hard (5×5) unlocks after 3 Classic wins, and Expert (6×6) after 5 Hard wins.

How are moves counted?

Every tile that moves counts as one move. Tapping a tile that slides a whole row of three tiles counts as three moves.

How do I stop crash reports?

Turn off Settings → Privacy → Share crash reports. See the Privacy Policy for details.
