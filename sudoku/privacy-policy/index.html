AN1 Sudoku

Privacy Policy

AN1 Sudoku · Effective October 11, 2026

In short:

  • There are no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your games, statistics and settings are stored only on your device.
  • If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the bug. Anonymous performance data (for example, how long the app takes to start) is sent to Firebase Performance Monitoring so we can keep the app fast. You can turn both off in Settings.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

Who we are

AN1 Sudoku (“the app”) is developed by Dmytrii Golovanov (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the data described in this policy. You can reach us at support@an1.tech.

Data stored on your device

To let you continue a game and see your progress, the app stores the following on your device:

  • the game in progress;
  • a history of finished games (the puzzle, result, date, time, score, mistakes and hints used), used to show your statistics and unlock levels;
  • your settings.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the app. It may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), depending on your device settings.

Crash reports and performance data

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report using Firebase Crashlytics. Since version 1.1, the app also sends performance data using Firebase Performance Monitoring. Both services are provided by Google LLC. We use this data only to find and fix bugs and to keep the app fast.

What a crash report contains

  • technical details of the crash (stack trace and time of the crash);
  • device and app information: device model, iOS version, available memory and storage, app version and whether the device is jailbroken;
  • random identifiers created for the app installation and session (a Crashlytics installation UUID and a Firebase installation ID), used to count how many installations are affected by a crash.

What performance data contains

  • how long the app takes to start, how smoothly screens are drawn, and how long it takes to create a new puzzle (with its difficulty level);
  • device and app information: device model, iOS version, locale, mobile carrier and app version;
  • the country the data was sent from, which Google derives from the IP address;
  • a random Firebase installation ID and session ID.

Crash reports and performance data do not contain your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos or the contents of your games. These identifiers are not linked to your identity and are not used to track you across other apps or websites.

Legal basis, processing and retention

We process crash reports and performance data on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working reliably and quickly (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google processes this data on our behalf as a data processor under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. Google may process it in the United States and other countries where it operates. For transfers from the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (including the UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework). Crash reports and the identifiers associated with them are deleted after 90 days. Performance data linked to an IP address is kept for 30 days, and other performance data for 60 days. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. See Privacy and Security in Firebase for details.

How to turn this off

Open the app, go to Settings → Privacy and turn off Share diagnostics (called Share crash reports in version 1.0). The app then stops sending crash reports and performance data and deletes any crash reports that have not been sent yet.

What we don't do

  • We don't require or offer accounts.
  • We don't show ads and don't use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics about how you play and don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.
  • We don't access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The only data that leaves the device is the anonymous crash reports and performance data described above, which can be turned off in Settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, the UK or California), you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its processing and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because crash reports and performance data are not linked to your identity, we usually cannot find the data that came from your device. You can always stop it by turning off Share diagnostics, and you can contact us at support@an1.tech with any request.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. If a future version of the app adds new features that use data, such as accounts or syncing between devices, this policy will be updated first.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@an1.tech.
