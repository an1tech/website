AN1 Sudoku

Classic Sudoku

Six difficulty levels, from Easy to Extreme. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and is graded by the solving techniques it needs. Games are saved automatically, and your statistics stay on your device.

Need help? Email support@an1.tech. Please include your app version from Settings → Version.

Questions

Where is my progress stored?

On your device only. Your current game, statistics and settings are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I unlock harder levels?

Easy, Medium and Hard are open from the start. Expert unlocks after 4 Hard wins, Master after 10 Expert wins, and Extreme after 14 Master wins.

Can I play without the mistakes limit?

Yes. Turn off Mistakes limit in Settings.

How do I stop crash reports and performance data?

Turn off Settings → Privacy → Share diagnostics (Share crash reports in version 1.0). See the Privacy Policy for details.
